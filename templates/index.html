Cyber Safety Tip of the Day General

The Golden Rule of UPI

UPI PIN is only used for sending money or checking your balance. You NEVER need to enter a PIN or scan a QR code to receive money.

If someone claims they are sending you an advance and tells you to scan a QR code, decline immediately.

Critical Citizen Protocol

The 'Golden Hour' Financial Fraud Directive

If you have been defrauded in an unauthorized banking, credit card, or UPI transaction, the first 2 hours are crucial. Reporting promptly to Helpline 1930 enables the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C) to trigger an automated inter-bank freeze across all downstream beneficiary accounts.

📞 Call Helpline 1930 (Toll Free) Open National Reporting Portal ↗
⚠️ Warning Against Secondary 'Fund Recovery' Scams: Never pay any individual, self-proclaimed cyber lawyer, or hacker claiming they can recover your lost money for an advance retainer fee. Only authorized banks, law enforcement, and courts can freeze or reverse fraudulent funds.
